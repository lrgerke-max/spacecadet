Meeting attention assistant

Spacecadet

You lost focus. You look over. Two seconds later you know what is being discussed — and whether someone just asked you something.

What it does

  • Listens to your microphone or the meeting's system audio and transcribes it in your browser.
  • Shows the current topic in text you can read from across the room, plus the last 30 seconds of speech.
  • Fires a loud alert the moment your name or a question aimed at you shows up. That runs on plain pattern matching, so it is instant and works with no account and no network.
  • Catch me up builds a prompt, copies it, and opens claude.ai — your own subscription pays, and this page never calls an API.

Where your audio goes

  • There is no server. This page is a single static file; nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere but your own browser.
  • By default speech recognition uses the browser's built-in Web Speech engine, which sends audio to Google to be transcribed. That is Chrome's engine, not this app's choice.
  • Want nothing to leave the machine? Settings → speech-to-text engine → Local Whisper, and set the summarizer to local. The app then blocks its own outbound calls and logs every blocked attempt so you can check.
  • The whole thing is one readable file. Open it in a text editor, or read it on GitHub, before you trust it with a microphone.

First run

  • Press S and enter your name — including whatever mangled spellings speech-to-text produces for it. The name alert cannot fire without this.
  • Press L and click Allow on the microphone prompt.
  • C catch me up · E export the meeting · Esc panic-hide the whole screen, again to bring it back.
  • For remote Teams or Zoom audio on Windows: switch the source to system audio, share the entire screen, and tick "Also share system audio". A window share carries no audio.
Open this page rather than a downloaded copy if you can. Served over https your browser remembers the microphone permission, so it stops asking at the start of every meeting. Opened from a file on disk it still works, but Chrome re-prompts every single time — that is a browser rule, not a bug.
Chrome or Edge. Built for Windows; macOS cannot capture system audio at all, microphone only.
